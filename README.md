This document is not a standard software README. It is a manifesto. It is a detailed explanation of why we built this, how it works, and how it will fundamentally alter your relationship with technology.

# Why JARVIS Exists

I built JARVIS because I was drowning in the sheer cognitive load of modern execution. 

As a founder, my day is a fractured mosaic of context switching. In a single hour, I pivot from reviewing product telemetry to writing strategic positioning documents, to debugging a React component, to managing a critical hire. Each of these tasks requires immense context. Each requires me to load a specific "mental state" into my working memory. 

When I turned to existing AI tools for help, the friction was immediate. Every interaction began at zero. I had to re-explain who I was, what my company was doing, what my design preferences were, and what the specific constraints of the current problem were. The AI had no memory of the architecture decision we made last Tuesday. It didn't know that we were running behind on Sprint 4. It didn't know that I was severely sleep-deprived and needed concise, actionable answers rather than a five-paragraph essay.

The existing tools were brilliant at answering questions, but they were entirely incapable of *sharing context*. They were oracles on a mountaintop—incredibly wise, but entirely disconnected from the reality of my daily life.

I realized that intelligence without context is just a textbook. True leverage comes from an intelligence that sits beside you, watches you work, remembers your past decisions, understands your current goals, and proactively organizes the chaos of your digital life. 

I needed a system that would not just answer my questions, but would *ask* me the right questions. I needed a system that would notice when I was procrastinating and gently push me back on track. I needed a system that remembered my failures and my successes. 

I couldn't find it. So I built it. 

JARVIS exists to close the gap between your highest ambitions and your daily execution.

# Core Philosophy

JARVIS is built upon a foundation of uncompromising principles. These philosophies dictate every architectural decision, every UI element, and every line of code in the repository. They are the non-negotiables of our product design.

1. **Context is King:** Intelligence without memory is useless. Every interaction with JARVIS must be contextualized by who you are, what you are trying to achieve, and what you have discussed in the past. If you have to repeat yourself, we have failed.
2. **Proactive, Not Reactive:** You should not have to ask JARVIS to help you. JARVIS should anticipate your needs based on your goals and your desktop activity. It should be an active participant in your workflow, not a passive search box.
3. **Local-First Privacy:** Your life's context—your goals, your fears, your startup's proprietary code—is the most valuable data you possess. It must never sit in plaintext on a cloud server. Privacy is not a toggle; it is the default state of the architecture.
4. **Emotional Resonance:** Software should not feel sterile. It should feel alive. JARVIS must possess a distinct personality, capable of humor, empathy, and tough love when you need to be held accountable. It must adapt its tone to your emotional state.
5. **Frictionless Action:** Talking to JARVIS should be as easy as thinking. The interface must be ambient, unobtrusive, and instantly available. If initiating a task takes more than three seconds, the cognitive friction is too high.
6. **Continuous Growth:** JARVIS is not a static product. It is a learning engine. It must continually refine its understanding of your preferences, your work habits, and your communication style.

# Memory System

The defining technical achievement of JARVIS is its Memory Intelligence architecture. This is what separates it from every other AI tool on the market.

When you speak to a standard LLM, your context window is a leaky bucket. Eventually, it forgets everything you said. 

When you speak to JARVIS, your input is passed through a background Memory Extraction Agent. This agent silently analyzes the conversation, looking for enduring facts: your architectural preferences, the names of your key investors, your dietary restrictions, your childhood memories, or your long-term goals.

These extractions are transformed into semantic vectors and stored in a local ChromaDB instance, heavily cross-linked with an SQLite relational graph. We call this the "Life Graph." It is a multidimensional mapping of your personal and professional context.

When you ask a question three months later, JARVIS silently queries this vector space before generating a response. It pulls your past context into its working memory. It feels indistinguishable from human recall. 

You can say, "Draft an email to Sarah about the new marketing push," and JARVIS will remember who Sarah is, what your last marketing strategy was, and the specific tone you prefer to use when communicating with her. You never have to repeat yourself. JARVIS simply *knows*.

# Privacy & Security

The most powerful software is also the most dangerous if compromised. Because JARVIS stores your proprietary code, your personal reflections, and your startup strategies, security is not a feature—it is the bedrock of the entire architecture. We assume a zero-trust environment.

1. **Application-Level Encryption:** Every piece of sensitive data inside JARVIS—memories, goals, reflections, and context logs—is encrypted at the application layer using Fernet AES-128-CBC before it is ever written to the SQLite database. If a malicious actor steals your `jarvis.db` file, they will find nothing but meaningless encrypted byte strings.
2. **Local Execution:** Your memory graph, your vector embeddings, and your entire database live strictly on your local hard drive. There is no cloud sync. There is no central server hosting your data. We do not have access to your memories. We cannot read your goals. 
3. **Secret Isolation:** API keys are never stored in plaintext and never exposed to the frontend React application. They are encrypted natively by your operating system (using DPAPI on Windows or Keychain on macOS) and injected directly into the secure Python backend at runtime. The UI renderer processes have zero access to your secrets.
4. **Explicit Consent:** JARVIS never continuously records your screen. Vision analysis and desktop capture are strictly triggered by your explicit command. We fundamentally reject the notion of passive, unconsented surveillance.

# Architecture Overview

Building a system of this complexity required rejecting standard web-app paradigms. JARVIS is built on a modern, deeply decoupled architecture designed for speed, extensibility, and security.

* **The Core Brain (Backend):** A high-performance Python FastAPI server. It handles local data processing, embedding generation, asynchronous task management via APScheduler, and real-time WebSocket event broadcasting.
* **The Orchestrator:** Powered by LangGraph, this is the cognitive routing layer. It evaluates user input, manages agent state, and dynamically routes the execution to the appropriate specialized agent (Executive, Memory, Research, Companion, Founder).
* **The Storage Layer:** A dual-database approach. SQLite handles structured relational data (Goals, Sessions, Logs), while a local ChromaDB instance manages the high-dimensional vector space for semantic memory retrieval.
* **The Experience Layer (Frontend):** A beautifully crafted React application bundled via Vite. We utilize Framer Motion for liquid, hardware-accelerated micro-animations, ensuring the interface feels premium and alive.
* **The Desktop Shell:** Electron serves as the native OS wrapper, managing secure IPC channels, deep OS integrations, window management, and hardware-level secret storage.
* **The Bundler:** PyInstaller compiles the entire Python backend into a standalone native executable. This entirely removes the need for end-users to manage Python environments, virtual environments, or complex dependencies. It just works.

# Installation Guide

JARVIS is currently in Public Beta (v1.0). We have invested heavily in ensuring the installation process is as frictionless as possible for Windows environments.

### Windows Installation Steps

1. Navigate to the `release` folder within the JARVIS repository or download from our official beta distribution link.
2. Download the `JARVIS Setup 1.0.0.exe` installer.
3. Run the installer. The process is fully automated. No administrative privileges are strictly required for the base installation, ensuring it can run in restricted corporate environments.
4. JARVIS will automatically install to your local `AppData` directory, unpack the bundled Python backend, and launch the application.

# First Run Experience

Upon your very first launch, you will be greeted by the **Welcome Wizard**. This is a secure onboarding flow designed to set up your core intelligence provider, lock down your local encryption, and establish your baseline profile.

### 1. Gemini Setup (Recommended)
For the highest level of reasoning, speed, and multimodal vision capabilities, we strongly recommend using Google's Gemini models (specifically Gemini 1.5 Pro or Flash).
* Select **Gemini** in the provider configuration screen.
* Enter your Google AI Studio API key. 
* JARVIS will securely encrypt this key into the Windows Credential Manager. It will never be stored in plaintext and will never be exposed to the frontend UI.

### 2. Ollama Setup (Local-Only)
If you operate in highly secure environments, require absolute air-gapped privacy, or refuse to send any data over the network, you can run JARVIS purely on local silicon.
* You must have [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) pre-installed and running on your local machine.
* Select **Ollama** in the provider screen. JARVIS will default to local models like `llama3` for its core reasoning engine. 
* *Important Note: Advanced vision capabilities and execution speed may be heavily constrained by your local GPU hardware. Expect slower response times and reduced multimodal accuracy.*

### 3. Hybrid Setup
This is the optimal balance for advanced users. JARVIS will use Gemini for complex reasoning, vision tasks, and deep context aggregation, while intelligently falling back to local Ollama models for background tasks, sensitive text parsing, and routine memory extraction to save API costs and enhance privacy.

### Recovery Key Generation

During the final stage of the Welcome Wizard, JARVIS will generate a 32-character **Recovery Key** (e.g., `A7B9-X2M4-9QPL-4RTV-8NWC-3KJH-5FDS-2YTR`). 

**You must write this key down and store it in a secure password manager.**

Because your SQLite database is fully encrypted at the application level, it is locked to your specific machine's secure credential store. If your machine's local keychain is corrupted, your OS is reinstalled, or you migrate to a new computer, you will lose access to all your memories and goals forever. This Recovery Key is the only cryptographic backdoor to decrypt your database. We cannot recover it for you. 

# Safe Mode

We recognize that granting an AI the ability to execute shell commands and control desktop applications is inherently risky. We have designed JARVIS to be powerful, but power requires control.

If you ever feel that JARVIS is behaving unpredictably, if you are working on highly sensitive production servers, or if you simply want to restrict its capabilities to a pure conversational agent, you can trigger **Safe Mode**.

Safe Mode can be toggled instantly from the JARVIS Settings panel or via a voice command. When activated:
* All external shell execution is hard-blocked at the middleware level. Any attempt by the AI to run a command will be rejected.
* Window management and active process inspection are disabled.
* Plugin execution is suspended.
* JARVIS falls back to a pure, isolated conversational state, safe for any environment.

# Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during installation or daily use, please check the following:
* **Backend Fails to Start:** Ensure your antivirus is not blocking the `jarvis_backend.exe` bundled executable. Some aggressive heuristics flag compiled Python binaries.
* **WebSocket Disconnections:** If the UI shows a "Reconnecting" state permanently, the backend may have crashed. Check the diagnostic logs located in `%APPDATA%/jarvis-desktop/logs`.
* **Vision Features Failing:** If you are using Gemini, ensure your API key has access to the vision models. If using Ollama, ensure you have a multimodal model pulled (e.g., `llava`).
